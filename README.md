At Providence, I worked across the product lifecycle and was recognized with multiple milestone awards for outstanding performance, strong technical ownership and leading 3 person team through delivery. Over the last year, I expanded into Microsoft D365 Power Platform, working on CRM-based solutions and building user-facing applications used by healthcare systems.<br><br>I recently moved to Bay Area to start the next chapter of my life.
